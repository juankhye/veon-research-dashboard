# VEON (VEON US) — First-Principles Research Dashboard

Single-file interactive research dashboard on VEON Ltd (Nasdaq: VEON), the emerging-market digital operator across Pakistan, Ukraine, Kazakhstan, Bangladesh and Uzbekistan.

Live: https://juankhye.github.io/veon-research-dashboard/

Sections: Telecom 101 · corporate history · interactive footprint map · country-by-country review · flywheel business model · digital deep dive · revenue and cost drivers · financials · FX sensitivity tool · geopolitical heat map · capital structure · spectrum, towers, energy and satellite · moat · valuation with interactive SOTP builder · bull vs bear · risk matrix · catalysts · glossary.

Sources: AlphaSense Company Primer and Bull/Bear (15 Sep 2026), Baptista Research (18 Aug 2026), ZeroOne 2Q26 review and financial model (Aug 2026), INAM (3 Aug 2026), Litchfield Hills (20 Aug 2026). Live prices from IBKR, 15 Sep 2026. Built with Chart.js, Leaflet and the TradingView widget; no backend.
